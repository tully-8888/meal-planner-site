Privacy Policy

Meal Planner is designed to keep your personal planning data on your device.

Data that stays on your device

Your dietary preferences, allergies, budget, pantry inventory, household details and generated meal plans are stored locally. We do not upload this information to our analytics, diagnostics or subscription providers.

Optional AI grocery estimates

If you enable AI price estimates, the app sends your selected country, optional city, currency, supermarket type and optional supermarket name, together with the generic ingredient catalog, directly to OpenAI to estimate ingredient prices. These requests do not include your dietary preferences, allergies, budget, household details, pantry, meal plan, or anonymous app identifier. This feature does not request precise device location. OpenAI also receives network information needed to process the request and may retain data under its API data policies. You can turn off AI estimates in Settings to stop new requests; meal planning remains available without prices.

Data we may collect with consent

If you opt in, we collect an anonymous app identifier, product interactions, app version, device class, operating system, locale and coarse performance information through Mixpanel. IP-based geolocation and automatic event collection are disabled. This data is used to understand onboarding, planning, grocery and subscription flows.

If you opt in to diagnostics, Sentry may receive crash data, performance data and technical diagnostics needed to fix reliability problems. Planner content and handled purchase cancellations are excluded.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier, product identifiers, purchase state and entitlement status to operate subscriptions. Mixpanel may receive analytics copies of subscription lifecycle events under that identifier. Mixpanel never grants access to paid features.

Advertising and tracking

We do not show third-party advertising, sell personal data or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Retention and choices

You can disable analytics or diagnostics in Settings. You can delete all local planner data from Settings. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat as required to provide purchases and meet legal obligations.

Contact

Questions can be sent through our support page.
