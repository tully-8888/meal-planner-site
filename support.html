MEAL PLANNER SUPPORT

How can we help?

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in Meal Planner and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Reset your plan

Open Settings and choose Reset local data. This removes preferences, plans, pantry and grocery state from the device. The action does not cancel a subscription.

Grocery price estimates

Optional AI estimates use OpenAI to estimate the ingredients used in your dinners. They are not live retailer prices; buying full packs can cost more.

Contact

Email topnotch.stdio@gmail.com with your app version and a short description. Do not send allergy, dietary, budget or pantry details.
