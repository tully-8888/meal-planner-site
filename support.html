MEAL PLANNER SUPPORT

How can we help?

Restore a purchase

Open Profile in Meal Planner and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Reset your plan

Open Profile and choose Reset Local Data. This removes preferences, plans, pantry and grocery state from the device. The action does not cancel a subscription.

Nutrition and prices

Nutrition and grocery costs are estimates. Meal Planner does not provide medical advice or live retailer pricing.

Contact

Email topnotch.stdio@gmail.com with your app version and a short description. Do not send allergy, dietary, budget or pantry details.
